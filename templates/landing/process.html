LIFECYCLE ARCHITECTURE

The End-to-End Procurement Process

Trace every stage of purchasing intent from department requisition initialization to final payment reconciliation.

01 Standard cycle: 2-4 business hours

Requirement & Budget Verification

Departments draft purchase requests with specified item lists, specifications, and project allocations. The system checks available budget allocations prior to submission.

Key Deliverable / Output: Approved Requisition with reserved budget allotment
  • Department user creates structured requisition
  • Automatic cost-center budget allocation check
  • Initial supervisor and department head approval routing
02 Standard cycle: 2-5 business days

Sourcing & Vendor Quotations

Procurement teams issue RFQ (Request for Quotation) packages to qualified suppliers. Suppliers submit quotations with pricing, delivery schedules, and technical catalogs.

Key Deliverable / Output: Comparative Bid Analysis & Supplier Selection Record
  • RFQ published to selected supplier categories
  • Supplier portal submission for sealed quotes and timelines
  • Side-by-side commercial and technical comparison matrix
03 Standard cycle: Same-day turnaround

Purchase Order Generation

Once a quotation is selected, a formal Purchase Order is generated with clear line-item descriptions, payment schedules, delivery dates, and terms of service.

Key Deliverable / Output: Dispatched & Confirmed Purchase Order (PO)
  • PO document generated with unique reference tracking
  • Digital dispatch to supplier via connected workspace
  • Supplier formal acknowledgement and delivery confirmation
04 Standard cycle: Upon delivery arrival

Shipment, Receiving & Inspection

Upon arrival of physical goods or completion of services, warehouse and operations teams inspect delivery quantities against PO specifications.

Key Deliverable / Output: Verified Goods Receipt Note (GRN)
  • Receiving team logs quantity count and physical condition
  • Quality checklist verification and acceptance record
  • Goods Receipt Note (GRN) created for matched items
05 Standard cycle: 1 business day

Invoice Review & 3-Way Reconciliation

Finance matches the vendor invoice against both the original Purchase Order and the Goods Receipt Note to confirm item quantities, rates, and totals match.

Key Deliverable / Output: Reconciled Invoice Package Ready for Payment
  • Line-by-line item rate verification against Purchase Order
  • Quantity received reconciliation against Goods Receipt Note
  • Variance detection flag for unexpected price or quantity deviations
06 Standard cycle: As per contract terms

Payment Settlement & Order Closeout

Accounts payable releases payment according to agreed payment terms, updates financial ledgers, and archives the completed order dossier for historical reference.

Key Deliverable / Output: Settlement Confirmation & Archived Order History
  • Payment scheduling according to vendor terms
  • Remittance advice generated and shared with supplier
  • Order status updated to Completed with complete document history
