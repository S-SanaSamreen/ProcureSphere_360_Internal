Approval Policy Engine

Configure multi-level routing by transaction type, amount, department and cost center. Policies are versioned: editing creates a new version and never re-routes work already in flight.

How routing works

For each transaction the most specific active policy applies: cost center, then department, then global; within a scope, the amount band that contains the value. Each step must be approved by a different person, and every approver is also bound by their personal approval limit. Exception route policies add their steps for requests raised as exceptions (budget overrun, PO above estimate, award to other than the lowest bidder).
